Tacos

Last made 6 days ago. Made 14 times. Serves 4.

+ PlanAdd to listCook
Kids want the cheese and sour cream on the side.

Ingredients

1 lbGround beef
1 packFlour tortillas
1 pktTaco seasoning
1 cupShredded cheddar
1Yellow onion

Steps

  1. Dice the onion and brown it with the ground beef over medium-high heat.
  2. Drain the fat, stir in the taco seasoning and ⅔ cup water. Simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or wrapped in foil in the oven.
  4. Serve with cheese, sour cream and toppings on the side.
